First-Time Setup Guide & One-Click Suite Installation

When you install Peace Launcher on any Android TV, you do not need to configure complex settings manually. The built-in setup wizard handles everything automatically:

1
Allow Unknown Sources
On your Android TV, open Settings → Device Preferences → Security & Restrictions and enable Unknown Sources for your file manager or browser.
2
Install Peace Launcher
Install TvHome-release.apk. Press the HOME key on your TV remote and choose Peace Launcher → Always.
3
Google Photos Auto-Sync
The Setup Wizard opens automatically on first launch. Enter your shared Google Photos album link (or scan the on-screen QR code from your phone) — or tap Curated Wallpapers for automatic 4K backgrounds!
4
1-Click Install All Apps
In Step 3 of the Wizard, click ⚡ Install All Apps. It will automatically trigger sequential installation of Peace Browser, SmartTube, and Peace File Manager without searching for APKs!

💡 3 Quick Ways to Sideload onto your TV:

  • Method 1 (Easiest - Direct TV Browser): Open Peace Browser or any browser on your Android TV, navigate to this webpage, and tap Download APK directly on the TV.
  • Method 2 (Via USB Flash Drive): Download the APKs or Bundle ZIP on your PC/Mac, copy them to a USB thumb drive, plug the drive into your TV, and open via Peace File Manager to install.
  • Method 3 (Via Phone): Install Send Files to TV on both your Android phone and TV. Download the APKs on your phone and beam them wirelessly to the TV in seconds.
